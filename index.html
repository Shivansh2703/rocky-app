Rocky

A training planner for iPhone. This page covers the Rocky app only.

Privacy policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

Rocky collects no data. Nothing you type or plan leaves your phone.

What stays on your phone. Your calendar, your class and work blocks, your planned sessions, and your log (done and how hard) are stored on your phone only. Planning runs on your phone with simple fixed rules.

No accounts, no tracking. Rocky has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no tracking. Rocky does not ask for any permissions. Rocky keeps a random ID on your phone and never sends it anywhere.

Deleting your data. Delete the app and your data on the phone is gone.

Children. Rocky does not knowingly collect information from anyone, children included.

Not medical advice. Rocky is a training planner, not medical advice.

Changes. If this page changes in a real way, the date above changes.

Contact. Open an issue at https://github.com/Shivansh2703/rocky-app/issues.

Support

Questions, problems, or ideas about Rocky: open an issue at https://github.com/Shivansh2703/rocky-app/issues. Replies usually come within a few days.

How do I change or undo a plan? Type the change in the chat box. Rocky previews the plan on your calendar with a small pill: Accept, Try another, or Cancel. Nothing changes until you accept. After Accept, an Undo button shows briefly.

How do I delete my data? Delete the app. Everything lives only on your phone.

Does Rocky need an account or a connection? No. Rocky works without either.
